DHARMIC CONCEPTS · MATERIALS
Why is bilva dear to Mahadev?

A hunter got lost in a forest, climbed a tree, and dropped leaves through the night to stay awake. He did not know there was a Shivalinga at the roots. He did not know it was a bilva tree.

Three leaves, one stem. The tree did not study scripture to grow this way. The tradition looked at what grew and recognised something it already knew.

DHARMA · 4/5 Shiva Purana · Bilvashtakam
THE PART NOBODY ELSE PUBLISHES
Corrections, not warnings

Every guide ends with the misconceptions attached to that ritual, and what the source text actually says. This is the section we will never remove.

✕"Nirjala or the vrat doesn't count."
✓No source text makes the waterless fast mandatory. Sajal and phalahar are recognised forms. A fast adapted to your health, kept sincerely, is complete.
✕"Only a pandit can do this correctly."
✓Nothing in the source tradition restricts household puja to priests. The founding Bilva narrative involves a hunter who knew no ritual at all.
✕"Every eclipse affects everyone in India."
✓Visibility decides applicability. Where an eclipse cannot be seen, Sutak Kaal is not observed. This is the fact most forwarded warnings skip.
